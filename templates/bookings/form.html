How pricing works

Leave the subtotal blank and it is priced from inventory: a hotel is the chosen room offer (or, if none is chosen, the cheapest active offer valid for every night, else the hotel's base price) × nights; a car the daily price × days; a package the price per person × travellers. Type a subtotal to charge a different amount (the chosen room offer is still recorded on the booking). Tax is added automatically at the BOOKING_TAX_PERCENT rate and the booking is saved as Pending Payment with a generated booking number and a no-login customer payment link, which is emailed to the customer when the box is ticked.

Find inventory IDs on the inventory search page.
